Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how Steeltree Labs LLC ("Steeltree," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with VIF: Punchlists & Reports (the "App"). It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

The short version

  • VIF has no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email, or password, and we never see your payment details.
  • Your projects live on your device and in your own iCloud account.
  • When you ask for an AI analysis, the photo or text is sent to OpenAI for processing. Neither we nor OpenAI use it to train AI models.
  • We keep a limited amount of data on our servers: AI request records and the PDF reports you create.
  • We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • We don't use facial recognition or try to identify people in your photos.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Content You Create

When you use the App, you create content such as projects, punch list items, photos, voice memos and their transcriptions, text descriptions, comments, mentions, assignees, location tags, and reports ("Your Content"). Photos may include information captured with them, such as the date and time, compass heading, and, if you allow it, location and weather conditions.

Your Content may include information about other people, for example people who appear in photos or the names of team members you add to a project. You are responsible for having the right to include that information (see Section 9).

1.2 Information Sent for AI Analysis

When you request an AI analysis, we receive the photo or text you submitted, any context you add, your analysis settings (such as language and level of detail), and technical information about the request. We store a record of each request, including the AI's response, processing status, timing, and cost. Photos sent for analysis are stored only temporarily (see Section 6).

1.3 Purchase and Subscription Information

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, email address, Apple ID, or payment card details. We receive information from Apple about your purchases and subscription, such as an App Store transaction identifier, subscription status, renewal and refund events, and token pack purchases. We use the App Store transaction identifier (or, if it is unavailable, a randomly generated identifier for your device) to keep track of your subscription and token balance.

1.4 Usage and Device Information

We collect technical and usage information, such as which features are used, token usage, app version, device type and operating system version, and error information. This is collected through our analytics provider, TelemetryDeck, and through our servers when you use AI features. It is linked to the identifier described in Section 1.3, not to your name. It does not include the contents of your photos or projects.

1.5 Device Permissions

The App asks for permission before using the following features. You can change these permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

  • Camera and Photos, to take photos and, if you choose, save exported photos to your photo library.
  • Location, to tag photos with location and to look up weather conditions. Weather is provided by Apple Weather, so your approximate location is sent to Apple for this purpose.
  • Motion sensors, to record compass heading and level when you take a photo.

1.6 Autodesk Build Connection

If you connect the App to Autodesk Build, you sign in directly with Autodesk. We never see your Autodesk password. The access credentials Autodesk issues are stored securely in your device's Keychain and are not sent to our servers.

1.7 Information Processed Only on Your Device

Some features run entirely on your device and do not send data to us, including natural-language search (using Apple's on-device intelligence features) and compass and level readings.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide the App's features, including AI analysis, reports, collaboration, and the Autodesk integration;
  • manage subscriptions, track token balances, prevent duplicate charges, and refund tokens for failed requests;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the App, and detect and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • understand how the App is used and improve it, including evaluating the quality of AI responses;
  • respond to your questions, requests, and support needs; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use Your Content to train AI models, we do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers that process information on our behalf and under our instructions: Supabase (database, file storage, and server functions), OpenAI (AI analysis), and TelemetryDeck (app analytics). Apple provides iCloud, purchase processing, weather data, and speech recognition under its own privacy policy.
  • People you share with. When you share a project, the people you invite can see its contents, including photos, comments, and reports.
  • Autodesk, at your direction. If you connect Autodesk Build and send items, that content is transferred to Autodesk and handled under the Autodesk Privacy Statement.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if we believe in good faith it is required by law or legal process, or necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or Steeltree.
  • Business transfers. If Steeltree is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law.

5. AI Processing

AI analyses are performed by OpenAI through its API. When you request an analysis, the photo or text and the related instructions are sent to OpenAI, which returns the results to our servers and then to the App. Under OpenAI's API data policies, OpenAI does not use this data to train its models, but may retain it for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, after which it is deleted unless the law requires otherwise. We do not use Your Content to train AI models either.

AI results are generated automatically and may be inaccurate. See Section 7 of our Terms of Service.

6. How Long We Keep Information

  • Photos sent for AI analysis are deleted from our servers after the request is processed, whether it succeeds or fails.
  • AI request records are kept as long as needed to operate billing, prevent duplicate charges, and troubleshoot, and are then deleted.
  • PDF reports are kept while they are available for you and your collaborators to download, and are deleted when you delete them or when you ask us to delete them.
  • Subscription and token records are kept while you have an active subscription or token balance, and afterwards as needed for accounting, tax, and legal purposes.
  • Analytics data is kept as long as needed to understand how the App is used and to fix problems, and is then deleted.
  • Data on your device and in iCloud stays under your control until you delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit, access controls on our servers, restricted access to stored files, and storing Autodesk credentials in the device Keychain. Data in iCloud is protected by Apple's security measures. No system is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee the security of information. You are responsible for securing your device and Apple ID.

8. Your Choices and Rights

8.1 Choices in the App and on Your Device

  • You can turn off camera, location, microphone, and speech permissions in your device's Settings. Some features will not work without them.
  • You can delete projects, items, photos, and reports in the App. Deleting them in the App removes them from your iCloud account.
  • You can stop sharing a project or remove participants at any time.
  • You can disconnect Autodesk Build in the App's settings. Content already sent to Autodesk remains in your Autodesk project.

8.2 Requests to Access or Delete Data on Our Servers

To ask for a copy of the data we hold on our servers, or for its deletion, contact us at the address in Section 13. Because VIF has no accounts, we will need information to locate your data, such as your App Store transaction ID, which you can find [in the App's Settings screen]. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. Deleting the App does not delete data on our servers or in your iCloud account.

8.3 Rights Under State Privacy Laws (including California)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to access, correct, or delete it; to obtain a portable copy; and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or of certain profiling. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use it for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You may use an authorized agent to make a request, and we may need to verify the request.

8.4 Rights in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Steeltree is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of your personal information, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We rely on the following legal bases: performance of our contract with you (to provide the App and process subscriptions); our legitimate interests (to secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights); your consent (for device permissions such as location and microphone); and compliance with legal obligations.

Your information is processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate, which may not have the same data protection laws as your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Information About Other People

VIF is used to document job sites, so photos and projects may include information about other people, such as workers who appear in photos or team members named in a project. When you add this information, you are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so and for giving any notice or obtaining any consent required by law. We process this information only to provide the App to you.

We do not use facial recognition and do not collect biometric identifiers or biometric information from photos. If you believe your information has been included in someone's VIF project without permission, please contact the person who created the project. You may also contact us, and we will help where we can.

10. Children

The App is a professional tool intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-Party Services

The App works with services operated by others, including Apple and Autodesk. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies, including Apple's Privacy Policy and the Autodesk Privacy Statement. We are not responsible for their practices.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the App before they take effect and update the effective date above.
